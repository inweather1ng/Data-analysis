# Data-analysis
Project Goals:
Develop a graph that models the inflow of Folsom lake with the recent Snow Water Equivalent trends. 
Train a Random Forest model to design a predivtive model to understand how the lake inflow ill change with snow, and how the lake storage will change as a result. These findings wil be usable for people liiking to measure the impacts of snow on reservoir conditions, adn will provide a baseline for water management descisions.
#Variables:
Snow water equivalent-Water stored in snowpakc during a specific time of year. 
Inflow-The amount of water flowing in to folsom lake at any given time
Storage-The amount of water retained in Folsom lake
EVAP-The amount of evaporated water durign a certain interval
Outflow-The amount of water released by the DAM.
